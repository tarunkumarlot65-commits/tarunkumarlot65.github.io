2. Community Rules

Not allowed on GlobeTalk:

  • Nudity, sexual acts, exposure of private parts, or sexual solicitation on video or in chat.
  • Anyone under 18, or any content involving minors in any way.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, abusive language, or encouraging self-harm.
  • Recording, screenshotting or sharing another person's video, voice or chat without their consent.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, impersonation, or collecting people's personal details.
  • Anything illegal under the laws of India or of your country.

What happens: messages with abusive or adult words are blocked and count as a strike; 3 strikes = permanent ban. Explicit video is automatically hidden for the viewer, the stranger is skipped and reported, and counts as 2 strikes. Serious or illegal content leads to an immediate ban and may be reported to the police. Automatic filters are not perfect, so please also use the Report button.

3. Privacy Policy

What we collect and why

  • Email and display name — to sign you in and show your name to the person you chat with.
  • One-time codes (OTP) — temporary, to verify your email.
  • Age check and selfie check — your date of birth is used to confirm you are 18+. The live selfie check runs in your browser; as currently built, your selfie image is not uploaded or stored by us. We keep only your verified status and a numeric match score.
  • Chat messages (text and its translation, language, sender name, time) — to deliver chat, handle reports and keep the service safe. Messages are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Wallet and gifts — your coin balance and gifts sent.
  • Safety records — reports, strikes and ban status, kept as long as needed for safety and legal reasons.
  • Technical logs — our hosting providers may log basic data such as IP address for security.

Video and voice. Video and audio go directly between the two users (peer-to-peer, encrypted in transit) and may pass through relay servers when a direct link is not possible. We do not record or store video or audio. Automatic nudity detection runs on your own device on the incoming video; no frames are uploaded.

Services we use. Hosting and database (Vercel, Render, MongoDB Atlas), email delivery (Resend), text translation (MyMemory), and your browser's built-in speech recognition and voice (in Chrome these may use Google's services). Text you want translated is sent to the translation service. If you view this page in a language other than English, the page text is sent to Google Translate to translate it.

Sharing. We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers above, and with authorities when the law requires or to report serious abuse.

Your choices. You can ask us to correct or delete your data or close your account by writing to the Grievance Officer below. Deleting your account removes your email, name and wallet; records needed for safety or legal reasons may be kept.

Children. GlobeTalk is not for anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has an account, we delete it.

Security. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes. We may update this page; the date at the top shows the latest version.
